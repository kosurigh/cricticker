How it works

Every unplayed fixture in a division is simulated tens of thousands of times to get each team's chance of finishing in each place. Margins are drawn from that division's own completed matches, so net run rate moves realistically rather than by assumption. Anything the page states as certain — clinched or eliminated — is proved from the points arithmetic across every possible remaining result, not inferred from the simulation.
